Create one pixel-art character sprite sheet for a tiny retro arcade game.

STYLE LOCK
Match the attached reference's look: chunky low-resolution pixel art, 1-pixel dark-brown/black outlines, limited warm jungle palette, gentle 3/4 top-down tilt, stair-step shading only, cute stout capybara with a rectangular head, blunt snout, and tiny rounded ears. TECHNICAL
- Regular 8-column by 2-row grid, equal cell sizes, even magenta gutters.
- Every sprite is designed to remain readable when reduced to exactly 16x16 pixels.
- Draw at 8x scale: each logical pixel is a sharp 8x8 block. No sub-pixel detail.
- One isolated capybara per cell, centered, feet near the bottom of the cell.
- No labels, captions, numbers, UI, watermark, or extra props except the tiny Zzz in the defeat cells.

7. Profile walk right, frame 1: stout body, small snout pointing right (true right-facing drawing, not a recolor).

ROW 2, left to right (first two cells only; remaining six cells empty magenta):

NEGATIVE
